## The sceptic's three (for the reviewer; never rendered)

1. “Repeated exposure without any requirement can leave a child on five foods for years.” Answered in the red lines and the panel: fewer than ten foods and shrinking, or weight loss, route to the doctor; the plan is for the common pattern, not the medical one.
2. “Letting a child spit food out teaches bad manners.” Answered in the menu: the putting-back bowl is a polite, planned way to end a taste, and it is what makes the next taste possible.
3. “Involving the child in cooking is a bribe in disguise.” Answered in the text: handling the food is a meeting with it, not a trade; nothing is promised for eating.
