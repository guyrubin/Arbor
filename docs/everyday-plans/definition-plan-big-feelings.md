| # | Field | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pattern id, title** | `plan-big-feelings`. EN "My feelings and my calm place". HE "הרגשות שלי והפינה הרגועה שלי". Names the place the plan gives the feeling, not the hitting. |
| 2 | **Shelf and age band** | Feelings shelf. Age bands `36m`–`6-8y`; register band 5–7. |
| 3 | **Parent words** | EN "Big feelings that overflow: hitting, throwing, screaming". HE "רגשות גדולים שעולים על גדותיהם: מכות, זריקות, צרחות". |
| 4 | **Body fact** | "When a feeling gets big, my body tells me first. My face gets hot, my hands squeeze tight, and my voice gets loud. Then, slowly, my body cools down again, like a kettle after the whistle." Picture: the kettle and the hot-to-cool band (PF2). |
| 5 | **The hard part, named** | "Sometimes the feeling gets so big that it spills out. My hands hit, my feet kick, or a toy goes flying. Afterwards, I feel wobbly inside. The storm came too fast, and I did not see it coming." Pose `hug-knees`. |
| 6 | **The plan, three steps** | Default: (1) notice the hot: my grown-up says our word and I echo it — `notice-the-hot`; (2) my calm place and what is in it, the grown-up comes with me, the candle breath — `calm-place`; (3) after the storm, we fix it: pick up, say it, start again — `fix-and-start-again`. Alternative slot 2: `hold-and-wait` (the grown-up stays near, keeps everyone safe, waits for the storm to pass). |
| 7 | **The box** | A soft cushion, my soft toy, a calm book, a squeeze ball, the candle breath. |
| 8 | **The win** | "Little by little, my body learns to feel the hot coming and to find my calm place. My body gets hot, then it cools down. When something spills, we fix it and start again. That is my plan!" Frame line: "{name}'s body knows the way back." |
| 9 | **Sources** | `aap-tantrums` (AAP HealthyChildren: temper tantrums) · `aap-discipline` (AAP HealthyChildren: disciplining your child — repair, warm consistent limits, no physical punishment) · `parent-programme-evidence` (the public evidence base of parent programmes: emotion coaching, calm-down steps, planned repair; no brand). |
| 10 | **Red lines (watchFor)** | hurts themself on purpose · has talked about not wanting to live · started suddenly after a frightening event · someone was hurt and needed care · outbursts last a long time most days and the child cannot calm down at all. |
| 11 | **Banned words for this pattern** | EN tantrum(s), meltdown, aggressive, aggression, naughty, bad behaviour, time out, ADHD, oppositional. HE התקף/התקפי זעם, אלים/ה, אלימות, ילד/ה רע/ה, שובב, פסק זמן. Plus the shared §5 lists. |
| 12 | **Pose list** | Six: `hands-up` (cover, win), `stand-tall` (p1, step 3), `hold-tummy` (p2, step 1), `hug-knees` (p3, alt step 2), `point-up` (p4), `sit-calm` (step 2). |
| 13 | **The sceptic's three** | (a) a calm corner is a time-out by another name → the child helped make it, the grown-up goes with them, never a punishment, swappable for staying near; (b) frequent, long, injurious outbursts may signal more than a parenting pattern → red lines, no condition named; (c) a three-year-old cannot notice the hot → the grown-up says the word first, the child echoes, the repair step carries the plan at any age. |
