# Definition — `plan-new-food`

Plan-book standard §1, the thirteen fields. Manuscript:
`app/src/lib/library/plans/patterns/planNewFood.ts`. Reviewer's yes: **not
recorded** (B-GA-37 pending, EN and HE). Hebrew: native review owed.

| # | Field | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pattern id, title** | `plan-new-food`. EN "The food on my plate". HE "האוכל שבצלחת שלי". Names the plate and the choice, not the refusing. |
| 2 | **Shelf and age band** | Food shelf. Age bands `24m`–`6-8y`; register band 5–7. |
| 3 | **Parent words** | EN "Refuses new food, eats the same five things". HE "מסרב או מסרבת לאוכל חדש, אוכל/ת רק את אותם חמישה דברים". |
| 4 | **Body fact** | "My tongue has tiny taste spots. When a food is new, the spots say: what is this? They learn new tastes slowly, a little at a time. The more times a food visits, the more my tongue knows it." Picture: the tongue drawn like a map (PN3). |
| 5 | **The hard part, named** | "Sometimes a new food comes to the table, and my tummy says no. It looks strange, it smells strange, and I want my same things. So I push the plate away, and I push it away again." Pose `hold-tummy`. |
| 6 | **The plan, three steps** | Default: (1) one new food sits on my plate beside my same things, nobody says eat it — `sits-on-my-plate`; (2) a tiny taste, when I want to, and I can put it back — `tiny-taste`; (3) the same food comes back many times — `comes-back-many-times`. Alternative slot 2: `we-cook-it` (the child handles the food before the meal). |
| 7 | **The box** | My own plate with a spot for the new food, a tiny tasting spoon, a little bowl for putting back, my helper apron. |
| 8 | **The win** | "Meal after meal, my tongue learns new tastes slowly. The new food sits on my plate, and I decide. Some days I look, some days I taste. My same things are always there too. That is my plan!" Frame line: "{name}'s tongue is learning." |
| 9 | **Sources** | `nhs-fussy-eating` (NHS: fussy eaters) · `division-of-responsibility` (the division of responsibility in feeding, a public method) · `repeated-exposure` (the repeated-exposure evidence in early childhood). |
| 10 | **Red lines (watchFor)** | losing weight or dropped off the growth line · chokes or gags on textures or trouble swallowing · fewer than ten foods and still shrinking · a food caused a rash, swelling, vomiting or breathing trouble · under one year old. |
| 11 | **Banned words for this pattern** | EN picky, fussy, ARFID, eating disorder, feeding disorder, underweight, clean your plate. HE בררן/ית, הפרעת אכילה, תת משקל, לגמור את הצלחת. Plus the shared §5 lists. |
| 12 | **Pose list** | Six: `sit-at-table` (cover, steps 1 and 3), `stand-tall` (p1, alt step 2), `open-mouth-wide` (p2, step 2), `hold-tummy` (p3), `point-up` (p4), `hands-up` (win). |
| 13 | **The sceptic's three** | (a) exposure without any requirement leaves a child on five foods for years → fewer than ten and shrinking, or weight loss, route to the doctor; (b) spitting out teaches bad manners → the putting-back bowl is a polite, planned end to a taste; (c) cooking together is a bribe → handling the food is a meeting, nothing is promised for eating. |
