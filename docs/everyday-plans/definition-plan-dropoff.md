# Definition — `plan-dropoff`

Plan-book standard §1, the thirteen fields. Manuscript:
`app/src/lib/library/plans/patterns/planDropoff.ts`. Reviewer's yes: **not
recorded** (B-GA-37 pending, EN and HE). Hebrew: native review owed.

| # | Field | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pattern id, title** | `plan-dropoff`. EN "The door opens again". HE "הדלת נפתחת שוב". Names the win (the return), not the crying. |
| 2 | **Shelf and age band** | Feelings shelf. Age bands `24m`–`6-8y`; register band 5–7. |
| 3 | **Parent words** | EN "Cries at the kindergarten or school door". HE "בוכה בדלת של הגן או של בית הספר". |
| 4 | **Body fact** | "Every morning, my grown-up walks me to the door. Inside are my friends, my teacher, and my things. My grown-up goes to work, and later, in the afternoon, the door opens again." Picture: the gate and the path (PD1). |
| 5 | **The hard part, named** | "Sometimes, at the door, my heart beats fast. My legs want to stay with my grown-up, and my eyes get wet. The goodbye feels very big. So I hold on, and I hold on." Pose `hug-knees`. |
| 6 | **The plan, three steps** | Default: (1) the same short goodbye every morning: hug, high five, the same words, then go — `goodbye-ritual`; (2) one small thing from home in my pocket — `thing-from-home`; (3) my grown-up says when the door opens again, in my words (after snack, after the garden) — `door-opens-again`. Alternative slot 1: `kiss-in-the-hand`. |
| 7 | **The box** | A little stone, a photo card of my family, a ribbon from home, a note from my grown-up. |
| 8 | **The win** | "Day after day, my body learns that the goodbye is short and the hello always comes. Our goodbye is short and the same, my thing from home is in my pocket, and the door opens again. That is my plan!" Frame line: "{name}'s door opens again." |
| 9 | **Sources** | `aap-separation` (AAP HealthyChildren: soothing your child's separation anxiety — brief consistent goodbye ritual, transitional object, honesty about the return) · `rapee-helping-anxious-child` (Rapee et al., *Helping Your Anxious Child*, public parent book: graded steps, parent as coach). |
| 10 | **Red lines (watchFor)** | hurts themself when upset · has talked about not wanting to live · started suddenly after a frightening event · distress most of the day, every day, for weeks · says something at the kindergarten frightens or hurts them. Any one routes to the staff and the doctor and offers no plan. |
| 11 | **Banned words for this pattern** | EN separation anxiety, clingy, attachment disorder, school refusal. HE חרדת פרידה, נצמד/נצמדת, סרבנות. Plus the shared §5 lists. |
| 12 | **Pose list** | Six: `hands-up` (cover, win), `stand-tall` (p1, step 2), `hold-hand` (p2, step 1), `hug-knees` (p3), `point-up` (p4), `sit-calm` (step 3). |
| 13 | **The sceptic's three** | (a) a fixed goodbye becomes a ritual the child cannot leave without, and a missed pick-up time breaks trust → the ritual is short, the parent keeps the named time; (b) door distress can mean something wrong at the setting or an anxiety disorder → red lines; (c) a transitional object is for toddlers → the object step is swappable, the pick-up step is the heart. |

Refrains: body "the door opens again" / "הדלת נפתחת שוב"; plan "our goodbye
is short and the same" / "הפרידה שלנו קצרה ואותו דבר". Hooks: p3 ("I hold
on.") and p4 end unresolved. Plates PD1–PD8, placeholders only. Gate §1:
fields filled, sources per step, reviewer's yes **owed**.
