# B-AIOPS-02 — Shared-family Live allowance (18 October 2026)

Builds on the 15 October Live usage observation slice. Accepted intent (GA backlog, Interpretation): Free 3 minutes, Plus and Family 20 minutes of realtime voice per family per day, shared across every parent, session and device.

## What is enforced

- `POST /api/live/token` reserves a slice of the family's daily allowance **before** the provider is touched. Family and plan are server-resolved (`ensureFamilyForUser`, `resolveEntitlement`); the client supplies nothing.
- A reservation is one minute by default (`LIVE_RESERVATION_MINUTES`, capped at 5; changed 28 Oct from 5) and is **non-refundable**. The provider token's `expireTime` is the reservation end, and `newSessionExpireTime` is one minute, so a minted token cannot be parked and spent later.
- Overlapping mints from any member of the family serialize on one Firestore document per family-day (`aiQuota/live_family_{familyId}_{YYYY-MM-DD}`, UTC day, the same boundary the observation slice splits on). Two parents cannot exceed the allowance together.
- Renewal is the next mint: it reserves the next slice only if allowance remains; a remainder under 30 seconds is treated as spent. The allowance resets at UTC midnight.
- Exhaustion returns a typed `429 { code: "live_resting", retryable: false }` with `X-Live-Quota-*` headers and **no `Retry-After`**. Nothing is minted. The client shows a calm "voice is resting" card (EN/HE), turns voice off, keeps the typed composer, draft and crisis path, and never reloads or falls back into a loop.
- A provider mint failure releases the reservation (server fact). A reservation-store failure refuses the mint (503), never fails open.
- Reconciliation: `/live/usage` reports (open/heartbeat/close) are recorded beside the reservation. They **never** credit the budget. By default an expired reservation is charged in full. `LIVE_RECONCILE_CREDIT=observed` (off) would charge observed time instead, bounded by the slice, only after provider expiry, and only for complete telemetry (clean close, no heartbeat gap).
- Admin/comp families (`ARBOR_FAMILY_*`, `ARBOR_PLUS_*`) resolve through the existing entitlement path to the paid 20-minute allowance. No new bypass exists.

## 28 October completion

- **Fallback path.** The browser loop (device speech-to-text → `POST /api/voice` → read-aloud) spends the same family-day allowance. After the crisis screen (a crisis turn is never metered or refused), each turn reserves 30 seconds before the model is called; when the response ends the server settles it to the spoken length it handled: the parent's words plus every released sentence at 14 characters per second. Exhaustion is the same typed `429 live_resting` with `X-Live-Quota-*` and no `Retry-After`; a store outage is `503 voice_budget_unavailable`. A server crash mid-turn leaves the turn unsettled and it is charged its full reserve at the voice deadline plus 10 seconds.
- **Timeout and caller-gone.** A provider that has not answered the mint within 8 seconds is abandoned and the slice released. A token minted after the caller hung up reaches nobody, so its slice is released too. Both are server facts, never client reports.
- **Client.** A spent allowance on any path (the Live mint, a renewal, a fallback turn) stops voice (no re-listen loop) and shows the resting line, its own polite status element where the thread ends, in the calm sky tint: "Voice is resting until tomorrow. You can keep typing, and Arbor is still here if something urgent happens." (EN/HE). It is never the failure card and never the connection-ended notice. The composer and the crisis door stay where they are, and the composer's "continue by voice" hint is hidden while voice rests.
- **Microphone first (critic round 1, P1-1).** Ask opens the microphone before any token is minted and hands it to the Live session (`requestLiveMicrophone`), so a refused, missing or stuck microphone never spends voice time. With one-minute slices, a socket that never reaches setup costs at most one minute, never a Free family's day.
- **Renewal (rounds 3–5): growing slices, retried, quiet seams, no lost exchange.** The first slice stays one minute (it protects a failed start). Every slice is renewed 30 s before it ends, never earlier (round 3 renewed a 10-minute slice right after the first exchange; withdrawn because that slice could not be refunded when a talk stopped early). The server sizes each renewal to twice the slice it renews, at most 8 minutes (inside the provider's ~10-minute connection lifetime) and at most what the family has left, CHAINED onto the end of the slice it renews. Every reserved minute is a minute the family can talk, but no reservation is refunded. A talk that stops is charged through the end of its current slice, or through the end of the next slice once that has been reserved (30 s before the current one ends). Slices grow 1, 2, 4, 8 minutes, then whatever is left, so the charge can reach about twice the time talked plus 2 minutes. Examples: 2:00 talked is charged 3 minutes; 3:30 is charged 7; 6:31 is charged 15; 9:00 is charged 15; 20:00 is charged 20. A 20-minute paid talk is 1 + 2 + 4 + 8 + 5 minutes, four seams; Free is 1 + 2 minutes, one seam. This is still strictly better than main, where one token reserved the whole 20 minutes. A renewal must name the caller's own usage session that reported "opened" and is still open (`renewOf`); it is accepted only in the last 90 s of that slice and once per slice, otherwise 409 `live_renewal_invalid` with nothing reserved or minted. A failed renewal is retried at 15 s and 5 s before the end (each attempt may take up to 30 s; the first slice to arrive wins, any other is discarded and reported as `failed`). A slice that arrives after the end is discarded, never applied. If none arrives, the talk ends calmly with the closing line "This voice talk has ended. Your conversation is still here — tap the voice button to carry on, or keep typing." (EN/HE), never the connection-ended notice. A provider `goAway` brings the attempts forward to the socket's announced end. A socket that closes while the next slice is on its way holds (microphone open, "connecting") for up to 15 s and swaps when the slice arrives. Sockets swap only at a quiet moment: no model turn open, screening or playing, no reply awaited, the parent silent for 1.5 s by microphone level and transcription. A swap is forced only in a socket's last second. Even then nothing the parent hears is cut: screened audio already playing plays out, a turn still being screened joins the new socket's history once released, and a reply still being generated (never heard, since audio plays only after its screen) is dropped and asked again of the new socket (`turnComplete: true`), as is a question the parent finished in the last 8 s that has no reply yet (older unanswered words, such as room noise, stay history only). Every slice's token carries the conversation as it stood when the talk began. Each new socket is seeded (`sendClientContent`) with every screened turn of the talk plus any words of the parent it has not heard. Verified renewal mints are outside the per-user hourly AI cap (`aiQuota.ts` `isLiveRenewalRequest`) and bounded by the family allowance. When the allowance ends a talk, the closing review says so first. EU-route slices no longer count against the AI Studio prepay.
- **Decision for Guy: the slice cap** (`LIVE_RENEWAL_MAX_MS`, one constant). With the shipped 8-minute cap, a 20-minute talk has about four seams, and the overhang (charged minus talked) of a talk that stops is up to about 8.5 minutes. With a 4-minute cap, a 20-minute talk has about six seams (1 + 2 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 1), and the overhang is up to about 4.5 minutes. Fewer seams cost more unused time for a talk that stops early; both stay inside the provider's ~10-minute connection lifetime.
- **Round 5: a failed provider mint does not use up the retry.** When the provider mint for a renewal fails (an error, the 8 s mint deadline, or a caller gone), the route releases that renewal's reservation, and the slice it was renewing becomes renewable again. The client's retries at 15 s and 5 s before the end can then succeed. A renewal that was delivered keeps its slice renewed once.
- **Renewal and the calm end (P1-2, superseded in part by round 3).** The Live client receives each slice's `expiresAt` and the family's `remainingMs`. While at least 30 seconds remain, it mints the next slice 8 seconds before the current one ends (the same pinned context, now including the conversation so far, and the same acknowledged notice), opens the next socket on the same microphone and playback, and swaps at a quiet moment (no model audio playing or buffered, the parent silent for 1.5 s) or one second before the end. The parent sees no seam. When less than 30 seconds remain, or a renewal is refused as resting, the conversation ends calmly at the slice end (`onAllowanceEnd`): the resting line, the voice review, no error. A provider close in the last 5 seconds of a final slice is the same calm end. A renewal that fails for another reason ends as a connection end at the slice end. Each renewal overlaps the old slice by up to 8 seconds, which is charged (non-refundable).
- **Cost outputs.** Delivered slices, settled fallback turns and resting refusals are counted per day in `usageRollup/{day}.live` and read at `/api/admin/overview` (`docs/ops/incident-alerts.md`, "Cost and budget outputs").
- **Tests.** Boundary (remainder under 30 s is spent; UTC reset; one-minute default, three Free slices, twenty paid), overlap (ten concurrent mints; mixed concurrent turns and mints from two parents), crash (unsettled turn charged in full; expired token charged in full), timeout (hung provider mint released), caller-gone release, retry (each retry is a new reservation; total bounded), fallback refusal after Live spent, crisis never refused; `lib/geminiLiveClient.renewal.test.ts` (supplied microphone used and released; renewal 8 s before the end with a quiet swap; forced swap before the end; calm end at the allowance; null renewal; early provider close of a final slice; failed renewal is a connection end); `components/tabs/voiceProviderNoticeGate.test.ts` (microphone before the mint, budget end never the connection notice, resting line separate from the failure card).

## Data

Family-level usage only: parent uids, millisecond counts and timestamps in `aiQuota`. No child data, audio, transcripts or names. `aiQuota` has no client rules match (Admin SDK only) and the existing TTL field `expireAt` applies. Account deletion already clears uid-keyed `aiQuota` meters; a family-day document outlives a member only as a count and is removed with the family-day TTL.

## Not proved by this slice (B-GA-26)

- Provider-billed audio minutes versus reserved minutes; the reservation bounds spend, it does not measure it.
- A socket that closes before setup keeps its one-minute slice: the server cannot tell a failed start from a session the client hid. A microphone refusal no longer reaches the mint.
- A renewal whose server reservation succeeded but whose response was lost is charged and, the slice being renewed once, cannot be retried: that talk ends calmly at its slice end. Renewal mints are outside the hourly AI request limit; first mints count against it.
- Plans are per parent: in a family with mixed plans, each mint and turn is checked against the caller's own allowance over the shared family-day total.
- Live production exercise of the 429 card on a signed-in EN/HE device, and whether Gemini closes an in-progress session at `expireTime` (SDK documents "may preemptively close"); the reservation is charged either way.
- Exhaustion itself is not an incident; Live unavailability and budget-store outages are (B-AIOPS-03).
