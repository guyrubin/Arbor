# Arbor

Parenting support. A clearer child record. Stories and play.

## The family experience

Arbor is a parenting and play companion: practical support for adults, a parent-controlled record of their child, and personalized stories and play. We are developing these connected experiences in a private beta.

Parent — find words and a manageable next step; choose an action and return to what happened.

Growth — keep observations across development, build continuity and prepare useful questions for a teacher or professional.

Kids — create or revisit a story, a familiar hero or a playful experience the child wants again. Parents control creation and access.

Parents choose what Arbor remembers and shares. Developmental references inform guidance and observation; assessment and treatment remain with qualified professionals.

## Three ways to explore a collaboration

Commercial: a small, attributed family cohort with clear activation, return, payment and cost measures.

Clinical: a defined review scope, named reviewer, documented sources and release decisions.

Editorial / academic: original stories or play, age-fit craft and a practical evaluation of family use.

We suggest a 20-minute fit discussion, followed by one scoped proposal. The initial family cohort hypothesis is ages 3–8. Product availability, rights, staffing, fees and local scope are confirmed before an experiment begins.
