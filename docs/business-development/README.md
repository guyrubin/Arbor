## Distinct workstreams

| Track | Required outcome | Measures |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial | Qualified, attributable family cohort/distribution | Meaningful D7/W4, paid charge/renewal, contribution and CAC |
| Clinical | Named scoped reviewer, source/claim register and case review | Critical/routine pass, coverage and unresolved material issues |
| Academic/content | Three original Kids experiences and bounded interaction/craft review | Deliverable quality, voluntary repeat and parent approval |
| Brand/ecosystem | A specific intro, right/content or measured channel | Agreed outcome; no general clinical endorsement shortcut |

Five **discovery P1**, not approved pilots: **Michal Dalyot Center, GrowHub, Goshen, Dorit Aram, Susie Allison / Busy Toddler**. Up to four active initiatives: one commercial, one clinical, one Kids package, one international discovery. Adler is a commercial reserve with own-club overlap; Tina Bryson is a bounded parenting/play review reserve. Peterson stays in its existing workstream. Good Inside and ParentData remain competitive Hold.

Three family jobs: recurring parent moment; quality story/play; professional-conversation preparation (reserve). Ages **3–8** and first HE operation in Israel are testable hypotheses. International discovery starts with EN interviews/message tests before choosing one qualified country.

Proposed cohort: **40 families — 15 Parent / 15 Kids / 10 Combined**. Proposed thresholds: **70% D7 activation per entry, 40% meaningful W4, 20% net paid D30 from the common offer, 60% first renewal when mature**. Kids: **50% voluntary repeat D14, 80% parent approval with 70% response**. These are learning/management thresholds, not clinical effectiveness or market-wide validation. Clinical review proposal: 40 routine +20 critical cases, 100% critical, ≥90% routine, zero unresolved material issues within the released scope.
