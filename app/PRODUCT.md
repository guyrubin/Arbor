# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Parents (primary register: calm, clinical-adjacent, trustworthy) tracking and nurturing their child's development over years; a separate child-facing Kid Mode register (avatar comic worlds) that never mixes with the parent register. Parents arrive in short, interrupted sessions, often one-handed on mobile, sometimes stressed by a hard moment. Hebrew (RTL) and English are equal first-class locales.

## Product Purpose

Arbor is longitudinal child intelligence: capture what parents notice, understand development through counts (never scores-as-verdicts), act on it today, and remember it as the child's story. One product, six surfaces. Success = a parent leaves every session with one clear thing understood or one concrete thing to do, and everything worth keeping saved in one tap.

## Brand Personality

Calm, credible, warm. "Executive clarity for parents": structured, visual, never dramatizing. Sapphire "2035" visual language: flat clinical surfaces, jewel-tone accents, editorial serif display over Nunito/Heebo body.

## Anti-references

- Kinedu/BabyCenter-style generic content feeds: identical for every family, dead-end articles.
- Gamified streak-pressure parenting apps; guilt mechanics.
- Clinical scoring dashboards: percentile verdicts, red/amber/green judgments on a child (hard firewall: counts, never verdicts).
- Pastel "baby app" cuteness in the parent register.

## Design Principles

1. Clarity + capture: every AI or content output lands as a structured, scannable card the parent can save in one tap.
2. Counts, never verdicts: development is framed as opportunity, never deficit; no diagnosis-shaped output outside gated clinical review.
3. Picked for this child: surfaces personalize from age and observed signals, and say why in one plain sentence.
4. The tool disappears into the task: earned familiarity, standard affordances, 150–250ms state motion, no choreography.
5. Both locales are the design: EN and HE/RTL ship together, transcreated, never translated as an afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on all text (ink tokens are pre-verified: --arbor-ink AAA, --arbor-muted AA); 44px touch floor (--touch-min); reduced motion honored globally via MotionConfig reducedMotion="user" + CSS guard; full RTL mirroring; decorative icons aria-hidden with text labels present.
