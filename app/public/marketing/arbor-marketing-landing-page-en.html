Seven ways of seeing the same moment.

Arbor draws on seven child-development approaches, and each one notices something different. An answer brings in the few that fit the moment, usually three, and you can choose one to lead.

  • What the behaviour may be asking for: closeness, safety, your attention.
  • How much to help, and when to step back.
  • Where they can choose, try and do it themselves.
  • What this age can and can’t do yet.
  • What surrounds the moment: family, school, friends, the day they had.
  • What a tired parent can do tonight. A good-enough response counts.
  • The skill still forming under the behaviour, such as waiting, finding words or planning.

Questions parents ask first.

Is Arbor therapy, or a diagnosis?

No. Arbor offers parenting support. It does not assess, diagnose or treat, and it is not a substitute for professional care. If you are worried about your child, speak to a professional. A care summary can help you prepare for that conversation.

What ages is it for?

Parents of children to age 12. The milestone map covers 2 months to 5 years, and Kids Studio’s games and makers are made for ages 3 to 8.

What happens after the 14 days?

Choose a subscription to continue using AI features. Your saved information remains accessible. There is no card at sign-up and no automatic charge, and starting the trial does not purchase a plan.

Is my child’s information used to train AI?

No, unless you explicitly turn that on. We do not sell your data and we do not show third-party ads. Data is stored in the European Union, and you can export or delete it at any time.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Arbor runs in your browser, on a phone or a computer, in English and Hebrew.

Can my partner use it too?

Yes. Co-parent sharing is free, with or without a subscription. Your partner can answer family-plan requests, mark an activity done and add a note. With Arbor Plus you can add up to six children.

Will Arbor keep pinging me?

No. Gentle reminders come at most twice a day, never in the quiet hours you set, and you choose which kinds you want. Family-plan reminders show in Arbor the evening before; no phone alert is sent.

What if something feels urgent?

Arbor is not an emergency service. Safety & urgent help is always one tap away, with words to say and helplines for Israel, the European Union and the United States. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.
